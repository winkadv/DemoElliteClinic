About Elite Clinics

Elite Clinics is a medical and cosmetic clinic group in Egypt's Delta with two clinics. Elite Clinic serves Tanta and Elite V serves Zagazig. Both offer laser hair removal, cosmetic treatments and nutrition with Elite Shot. Dental care is available in Tanta only.

Questions, answered

Where can I get laser hair removal in Tanta or Zagazig?

Laser hair removal is available at both Elite clinics: Elite Clinic in Tanta and Elite V in Zagazig. It is Elite's signature service.

What is the difference between Elite Clinic and Elite V?

Both belong to Elite Clinics. Elite Clinic serves Tanta and Elite V serves Zagazig. Dental care is offered at Elite Clinic Tanta only.

Is dental care available at Elite V Zagazig?

No. Dental care is available at Elite Clinic in Tanta only.

Which services does Elite offer?

Laser hair removal, cosmetic treatments, nutrition and weight management including Elite Shot, and dental care in Tanta.

What is Elite Shot?

Elite Shot is part of Elite's nutrition and weight management programs, available at both clinics.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online: choose your clinic, your service and a preferred time, and the Elite team will call you to confirm.
